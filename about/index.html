About AlumniEmail

AlumniEmail is a guide to lifetime alumni email forwarding for universities. The Forward Email team writes and maintains it, and every claim about Forward Email links to a page on forwardemail.net.

Who runs this site?

The Forward Email team runs AlumniEmail. Forward Email is an open-source email service for custom domains. It supports IMAP, POP3, SMTP, CalDAV and CardDAV, and it can be self-hosted with Docker. Its founder was one of the first employees at DuckDuckGo.

This site explains one use of Forward Email: lifetime alumni addresses on a university domain. It is not affiliated with any university. University names appear only where Forward Email's published case study names them, and no university has reviewed or endorsed this site.

Where do the numbers come from?

Prices for Forward Email come from the pricing page and the Forward Email FAQ. University names, alumni counts and the Google, Microsoft and Exchange cost figures come from the case study. Each page links to its source next to the claim. Vendor prices change, so confirm current figures before you budget.

How do I contact the team?

  • About Forward Email for your university: email support@forwardemail.net.
  • About this site: open an issue on GitHub.
  • Security reports: email security@forwardemail.net, as the Forward Email security policy describes. This site also publishes a security.txt file at /.well-known/security.txt.

Source code

The site's source is public at github.com/forwardemail/alumniemail.com. Pages are written in Markdown under content/ and turned into static HTML by a small Node.js script with no dependencies. Every page also has a Markdown copy at index.md in the same folder, and /llms.txt lists them for AI tools.

Privacy on this site

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. Fonts, styles and images load from this domain only. The one script on the site runs the cost calculator in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Requests to alumniemail.com pass through Cloudflare, which runs as a CDN and proxy in front of GitHub Pages and processes the IP address of each visitor (Cloudflare privacy policy). GitHub Pages hosts the files and logs the IP address of each visitor for security purposes (GitHub Pages documentation); the GitHub Privacy Statement covers that data. For Forward Email's own service, see the Forward Email privacy policy.
