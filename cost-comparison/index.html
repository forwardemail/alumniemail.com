Alumni email cost comparison

Forward Email's Enterprise plan costs a flat $250 per month, or $3,000 per year, for unlimited domains and aliases. Per-user suites charge for every account, so their cost grows with each graduating class.

What does Forward Email cost a university?

The Enterprise plan is $250 per month for unlimited domains, quoted and invoiced directly. It includes everything in the Team plan plus unlimited API requests, a terms exception for third-party use such as alumni, a countersigned agreement and DPA, a dedicated support chatroom and onboarding help. Aliases have no per-user fee. Source: Forward Email pricing and Enterprise license details.

Forwarding needs no mailbox storage. If you also give alumni IMAP mailboxes, paid plans include 10 GB of pooled storage, and each additional 10 GB costs $3 per month.

Costs that are not on the invoice

Per-user mailboxes also carry work that forwarding removes. The case study lists the problems universities face with full alumni mailboxes: per-user licenses that grow with every class, administration of thousands of accounts, security exposure from dormant accounts and growing storage needs. With forwarding there is no alumni mailbox to secure or store, and new aliases can be created by the API as part of graduation.
